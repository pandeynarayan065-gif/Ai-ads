The sequence

DAY 0

First message on WhatsApp (India) or email (US), plus an IG DM if they post often. Set status to Contacted and the follow-up to day 3.

DAY 3

One nudge on the same channel: “Hi, just following up on this. Happy to send the concept.” Status Follow-up.

DAY 5

Top 10 of the week only: send the free concept ad of their real product (6–10 s, about $1 to make). This is the message that gets replies.

DAY 10

Polite close by email: “Closing the loop. If ads aren’t a priority now, no worries, the concept’s yours.” Then No or park it.
