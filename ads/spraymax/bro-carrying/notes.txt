3D shots: video/blender/carry_shots.py (Cycles CPU, v2: 12 samples + OpenImageDenoise, rendered at 75% = 810x1440
and upscaled to 1080x1920 in the edit; Standard view transform so the measured reference colours land exactly). Real label: video/blender/label_hires.py (4x rebuild, wordmark redrawn
from logo-wide.png). Render times on this 4-core box: 5-23 min per shot, ~1.5 h for all nine.

  python3 video/blender/label_hires.py
  <venv with bpy>/python video/blender/carry_shots.py --shot C1 --out FRAMES --samples 12 --res-scale 75
  (repeat for C2..C9)
  python3 ads/spraymax/bro-carrying/assemble.py --renders FRAMES --out draft.mp4 [--music temp.wav]
  # with the real hook (bottle held to the lens at 12.3 s in the clip):
  python3 ads/spraymax/bro-carrying/assemble.py --renders FRAMES --out final.mp4 --hook phone.mp4 --hook-end 12.3

Fixes made during the render (each found by checking frames, re-rendered):
  - backdrops: a bright emissive wall lit the bottle flat and filled the frame -> near-black void with one
    tight glow, invisible to diffuse light (the reference's look)
  - C4: the salt's backlight showed as a white panel (lights are camera-visible in Blender 5) -> all lights
    hidden from the camera; the chunk hovered over its reflection -> rests on the floor
  - C2/C6: 100 mm at 20 cm gives ~1 mm depth of field; focus was 1.6 mm off -> focus solved on the wordmark /
    the collar
  - C5: one bubble stream was off-frame -> streams moved inside the 70 mm-wide frame

Music: the reference's track is a trending Instagram sound. Post draft-v4.mp4 (SFX only) and pick the sound
in Instagram so it's licensed; draft-v4-temp.mp4 exists only to judge the cut timing.

v2 (shot-for-shot copy of the reference), changes after review:
  - fine-mist pump never drips: C3 fires a mist burst, C6 is a clean profile (theirs drip: a serum pump)
  - spray: translucent, uneven sub-jets, a few heavier droplets, swirl, blooms wide at the end
  - motion copied from the reference with optical flow: hard cuts, slow creep, a quick pull-back on
    frames 3-7 of C1, zoom jumps on the half-beat inside C3/C5/C9 (6.45, 8.13, 11.15 s)
  - finishing: highlight glow, chromatic fringing, filmic shoulder + S-curve, vignette, grain (no streaks:
    they ghosted the bright bottles and the reference has none)
