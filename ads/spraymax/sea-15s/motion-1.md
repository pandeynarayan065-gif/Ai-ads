Real slow-motion surf footage, shot at 120fps and played back at 24fps. The wave keeps pitching forward: the thick lip throws out and pours down into the curl, then explodes into white foam; offshore wind blows a veil of spray back off the crest; the foreground chop rises and falls. Camera: ARRI Alexa 35 in a water housing, held by a surf cinematographer at the waterline, 24mm lens, f/5.6, 180-degree shutter; gentle natural bobbing with the swell and a slow drift backwards as the wave approaches. Natural daylight, true-to-life colour, realistic water physics, no glow, no golden tint. Sound: only the roar of the wave and the wind, no music, no voices. No people, no text.
