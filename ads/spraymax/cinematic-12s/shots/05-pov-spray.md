## Prompt line

> First-person view, hands only, framed as in Image 6. One hand holds <Subject 1> upright with the overcap off; the index finger presses the pump once and a fine cone of mist leaves the nozzle hole in the side of the pump head and drifts sideways onto the open palm of the other hand, settling as tiny glinting droplets. Natural skin, cold light from above. The camera holds its distance, gently handheld.

## Pass criteria

1. One camera move at most; a hard cut at 6 s, no morph into the next shot
2. Mist only: airborne droplets, no stream, no drips
3. Blue light, never purple; no light fixtures, no glossy studio floor, no text other than the label
