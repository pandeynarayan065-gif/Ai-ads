Real expedition footage, filmed handheld from directly behind a mountaineer standing on the summit ridge of K2, about 8,000 metres up. Close shot from behind at shoulder height: we see only the back of his head and his shoulders in a red down suit with black backpack straps, and beyond him a sea of cloud and the jagged Karakoram peaks under a dark-blue sky.

In one movement he pushes his red hood back with a black-and-red mitt, uncovering thick, dark, messy, textured hair with a dry matte finish, not wet and not glossy. A hard gust hits: his hair lifts and moves in the wind, strands separating, yet it keeps its volume and shape and settles back into its messy texture when the gust drops. Spindrift streams past him.

He keeps looking straight ahead at the horizon the entire time. He never turns his head; his face is never visible, only the back of his head. Low, hard sun from the side rims the edges of his hair with light.

Natural cold daylight, true colour, no golden or warm tint, bright snow close to clipping. Real hair with individual strands and natural flyaways. Slight handheld movement, real-time speed, no slow motion, no cinematic effects. Sound: roaring wind and the rustle of the down suit, no music, no voices. No faces, no text.
