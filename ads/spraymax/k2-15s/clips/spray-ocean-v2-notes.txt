Why a new tool (Narayan on the first lock: "the label is literally just a jpg sticker and doesn't fit the bottle
well", "the bottle has a yellowish tint"):
- the first lock (label_lock_upright.py) lit the label with a smoothed column x row model: ~15% darker and flatter
  than the plastic around it, the band stopped short of the outline (cream strips both sides), and Veo's
  cream/yellow plastic (Lab b* ~142 vs the clouds' ~128) stayed.
- now the label is lit by Veo's own bottle luminance pixel for pixel (filled under its print), the wave band
  wraps to the outline, the label follows the bottle's perspective taper (left edge 548 -> 554 px down the label),
  and the whole bottle incl. pump is re-centred on the scene's white (paper b* 142 -> 132, half the local bounce
  light kept). Painting is limited per frame to where Veo's bottle actually is (grown from its middle over its
  own cream/print), so fingers, hair and the sky between them are never painted over.
Stability (label region, mean abs change frame to frame): Veo 5.68 (max 22.6), new 3.09 (max 6.4), no pops.
(The first lock's command and the tool options it needed are in label_lock_upright.py's docstring.)
