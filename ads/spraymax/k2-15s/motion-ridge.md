Real expedition documentary footage filmed from a helicopter near the summit of K2, about 8,000 metres up. A lone climber in a red down suit and black-and-red mitts traverses a knife-edge snow ridge, small in the frame, seen from above and behind, moving slowly and leaning into the wind, a fixed rope running along the crest beside him. Strong wind tears spindrift off the ridge in long white plumes that catch the sun. Dark-blue high-altitude sky; jagged granite peaks of the Karakoram and a sea of cloud far below. The helicopter drifts slowly toward the climber with the small constant vibration and slight wobble of a real aerial camera; no fast moves, no orbit. Natural hard daylight, true colour, no golden or warm tint, bright snow close to clipping, crisp blue shadows. Real-time speed, no slow motion, no cinematic effects. Sound: roaring wind only, no music, no voices. No faces, no text.
