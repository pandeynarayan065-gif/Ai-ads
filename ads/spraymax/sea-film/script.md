# Spraymax: "Before the mountains" (30s, 9:16, 24fps)

Reference: Giorgio Armani Acqua di Giò (Profondo 2023, Essenza 2012).
What we take from them: one element as the metaphor (the sea), black-and-white silver
grade, slow motion, the body moving with the water, long dissolves and double exposures,
almost no text, one brooding music track, and the product placed inside the element at
the end (the bottle in the surf).

**Idea:** Spraymax is a Himalayan *sea salt* spray. The Himalaya was once the floor of an
ocean. So the sea is the metaphor: water shapes the hair, the salt gives it texture, the
waves become mountains, and the bottle carries what the sea left behind.

**Rule:** no human face anywhere. Hair, back, shoulders, silhouettes only.

## Edit

- Grade: black and white, silver, deep blacks, film grain. Product shot muted, blue label only colour.
- Long dissolves and double exposures between shots (Essenza style); one hard match cut at 0:09.
- Type: thin serif, wide letter-spacing, white, small, centred. 0.8s fades. No animation.
- Music (Suno): dark, slow, brooding guitar with a deep pulse, 72 BPM, builds to 0:19,
  drops at 0:21, resolves on the product. Instrumental.
