A readable label needs letters ≥ 25 px → bottle ≥ ~280 px tall. That's impossible in a hero shot, so:
- In-hand bottle: label turned toward his leg (white bottle, ribbed collar, clear cap, edge of blue band only). No tiny garbled text.
- Real packshot (`brand/spraymax/assets/bottle-front-render.jpg`) is composited by the designer bottom-right.

## Look
- Palette: steel-grey clouds, white snow, the brand's deep indigo on the long coat (replaces the red cape, so no superhero IP).
- Product benefit: thick, textured, matte hair that holds its shape in the wind.
- Grain and atmosphere like a film poster; this one is meant to look epic, not like a phone photo.
