## Global look (inherited by every shot)
- Camera: ARRI Alexa 35, Super 35 sensor, 24 fps, 180-degree shutter, real time throughout (no slow-mo, no speed ramps). Prime lenses: 100 mm macro, 50 mm, 35 mm, 24 mm, all with gentle natural falloff.
- Palette in named tones: matte black fleece, charcoal-grey ash, ash grey overcast sky, bone white print. One warm accent only: ember orange in shot 8 (blurred foreground embers plus a faint warm low side glow on the skull). Nothing else is warm.
- Light: flat low grey overcast daylight (large soft top light, no hard shadows) in every outdoor shot. Exceptions: shot 7 backlit through white smoke (still daylight-grey, no night); shot 8 the same overcast but one stop darker with the warm ember glow. No night, no dusk, no blue-black sky anywhere.
- Contrast: low to medium. Blacks stay textured (fleece nap visible, not crushed). Whites stay bone, not paper white. Desaturated, cool-neutral grade, one fixed grade across the film (shot 8 is the same grade, exposed one stop under).
- Grain: natural 35 mm-style film grain, gentle lens falloff. No plastic sheen, no over-sharpening, no digital glow, no halo behind the product, no lens flares, no visible light fixtures, no mirror or reflective ground.
- Negative space: the burnt ground and sky are mostly empty. Shot 10 keeps the right 60% clean grey sky (tagline and url are post).
- Surfaces: the hoodie is matte black cotton-look fleece with matte screen print. Ash is dry charcoal-grey flakes and fine dust, not snow. Say "matte" every time. An unspecified material defaults to gloss, so never leave one unspecified.
- Wind: from frame left, steady, in every outdoor shot.
- Faceless throughout: from behind, hands only, or cropped at the collarbone. Boots plain black, no logos.
