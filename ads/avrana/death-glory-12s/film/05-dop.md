(No 04-director.md existed at time of writing; storyboard timings used as-is: 1.4 / 1.0 / 1.0 / 1.4 / 1.0 / 0.9 / 1.2 / 1.1 / 1.2 / 1.8 s. Re-check if the director changes pacing.)

## Global look (inherited by every shot)
- Camera: ARRI Alexa 35, Super 35 sensor, 24 fps, 180-degree shutter, real time throughout (no slow-mo, no speed ramps). Prime lenses: 100 mm macro, 50 mm, 35 mm, 24 mm, all with gentle natural falloff.
- Palette in named tones: matte black fleece, charcoal-grey ash, ash grey overcast sky, bone white print. One warm accent only: ember orange in shot 8 (blurred foreground embers plus a soft warm side glow on the skull). Nothing else is warm.
- Light: flat overcast daylight (large soft top light, no hard shadows) for shots 1-6 and 9-10. Exceptions: shot 7 backlit through smoke, shot 8 night with a warm side glow.
- Contrast: low to medium. Blacks stay textured (fleece nap visible, not crushed). Whites stay bone, not paper white. Desaturated, cool-neutral grade, one fixed grade across the film.
- Grain: natural 35 mm-style film grain, gentle lens falloff. No plastic sheen, no over-sharpening, no digital glow, no halo behind the product, no lens flares, no visible light fixtures, no mirror or reflective ground.
- Negative space: the burnt ground and sky are mostly empty. Shot 10 keeps the right 60% clean grey sky (tagline and url are post).
- Surfaces: the hoodie is matte black cotton-look fleece with matte screen print. Ash is dry, charcoal-grey flakes and fine dust, not snow. Say "matte" every time. The default for an unspecified material is gloss, so never leave one unspecified.
- Wind: from frame left, steady, in every outdoor shot.
- Faceless throughout: from behind, hands only, or cropped at the shoulders. Boots plain black, no logos.

## Practical effects list (what the crew must build)
- Overhead rig and a flat ash bed (charcoal-grey wood ash and fine dust, not snow) for shot 1, with a rig to drop the hoodie from above frame so it lands flat and puffs the ash.
- Air mover on frame left for a steady gust (shots 2-6, 9, 10), with a manual burst control for shots 4 and 6.
- Motion-control slider with a low tripod for the macros (shots 2-4), plus a dolly or slider for shots 7 and 8.
- Burnt ground set (charred earth, ash) with one bleached antler as a prop (shot 3). This is a prop only. No skull print there.
- Smoke machine (white smoke, no haze colour) for shot 7, plus a backlight lamp off-frame and a front bounce card.
- Night rooftop set for shot 8: fire barrel off frame close to the lens, for real embers that drift up. Hidden warm tungsten side source. The garment is kept clear of the embers.
- Handheld operator and a grey ash slope for shot 9. Plain black boots with no logos.
- Wardrobe: one hoodie, same one in every shot (a lying-flat copy for shots 1, 2, 4), plain black cuffs and no drawstrings, no zip, no other logos [VERIFY on the real garment]. Faceless performer for shots 5, 7, 8, 9, 10.
