# 02 Story: avrana / death-glory-12s

## The one feeling
Defiant, cold, risen from ruin.

## Hook (0–1.5 s)
Top-down, locked-off, flat overcast light: the black Death & Glory hoodie drops from just above frame and lands flat on a bed of charcoal-grey ash, chest print up, and a ring of ash puffs out from under it. A real impact and a real burst. It shows the product at once and works with the sound off. Source: research idea 1. First-frame fallback: ref 05.

## Beats
Palette: black, ash grey, bone white (the prints), one warm accent (ember orange, beat 8 only). Everyone faceless. Real time throughout, no slow-mo.

## Risks / open questions
- Audience and banned words are [VERIFY] in the brand file; I assumed young streetwear buyers who care about meaning and art in what they wear.
- Rebirth theme vs before/after: the ash-to-skull reveal depicts the design's own meaning, not a product result. No person or body is shown changing. Keep it that way in the prompt.
- Product lock says "white print", but the skull is a shaded, distressed illustration. Skull may gain extra antler points (Image 2 is the only source). Check every frame.
- Beat 1 may crumple the print or fold a sleeve over it. Fall back to a 4 s first-frame insert from ref 05 (`--aspect adaptive`).
- Fake text risk in beat 2 (filigree) and beat 7 (script half-hidden in smoke). Plan to cut, not fix.
- Face leak in beat 7. Hands in beat 5 (under 1 s, no rings).
- Ash may render as snow. Say "charcoal-grey ash flakes".
- Embers must never land on the hoodie, and the hoodie is never shown burning (beat 8).
- Drawstrings: none are visible in the photos. Confirm on the real garment [VERIFY].
- Up to 4 refs: only 3 are used here. The known limit is 1–3, so 3 is the cap.
