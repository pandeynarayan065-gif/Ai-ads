## Recommended picks
Ten shots, ~1–1.4 s each, ending ~1.5 s in real time. Arc: what remains → what's underneath → the decision →
rising → standing.
1. **#1 Drop into ash** (0–1.4 s). A physical hook: impact and an ash burst.
2. **#3 Filigree macro, ash settling.** Texture, front print without letters.
3. **#4 Antler in ash.** The "ingredient" beat, which makes the skull land harder.
4. **#2 Gust uncovers the skull.** The design's own sentence on screen; the film's centre.
5. **#6 Hood up.** The turn from object to wearer: the decision.
6. **#10 Cuff in the wind.** A fast texture breath between two figure shots.
7. **#8 Emerges from smoke** (swap for **#11 POV** if face risk is too high).
8. **#9 Embers on the rooftop.** The warm accent, once.
9. **#7 Climbing the ash slope.** Momentum into the end.
10. **#14 Hero on the ridge.** Real time, left of centre, room for "Create Yourself" + avrana.co in post.

Refs for the run: Image 1 = ref 01 (front), Image 2 = ref 02 (back skull), Image 3 = ref 04 (worn back, hood up,
for how it hangs). Ref 05 is for a first-frame insert of #1 if the multi-shot run crumples it.

## Open questions [VERIFY]
1. Drawstrings: none are visible in any official photo. Confirm on the real garment before the prompt says "no
   drawstrings".
2. Fabric, weight, fit and print method are not stated by Avrana; the film shows them without naming them.
3. Product lock says "white print". The skull is a shaded, distressed illustration with about four points per
   antler. Update the lock text so the model doesn't flatten it? (Count the points on the real garment.)
4. Is fire/ash/antler imagery on-brand for Avrana? It's the design's own theme ("destruction", "rebirth"), but the
   hoodie itself must never be shown burning.
5. Price differs by size ($60/$62/$64) against the brand file's $60: update the brand file.
6. Four refs in one request: our known limit is 1–3 (rulebook §2). Keep 05 out of the `--ref` set unless confirmed.
7. Ash vs snow: Seedance may render grey ash as white snow. Say "charcoal-grey ash flakes" and check the first frame.
