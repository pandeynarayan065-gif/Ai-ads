## What makes this ours (one paragraph)
The film acts out the design's own sentence: "From what remains, something extraordinary emerges." The empty hoodie falls face-down into ash and is veiled. The wind clears the antlered skull off this exact print. A faceless wearer pulls the hood up, walks out of the smoke past the one warm ember glow, and climbs to stand on the ridge. The match from printed antler to real antler to skull, and ash that won't bury the design, belong to this hoodie alone. It is one cold world of ash, black and bone white. Nobody shows a face, and the model writes no words.
