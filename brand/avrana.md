## Business
- Streetwear label: hoodies, sweatshirts, shirts/tees. Site: **avrana.co** (Shopify). Socials: @avranaco (Instagram,
  X), @avranaofficial (TikTok). Contact: on the site.
- Tagline on every page: **"AVRANA - Create Yourself"**. Home page lines: "Express yourself", "Loved for style",
  "It's hard to be nice if you don't feel comfortable.", "Feel trendy", "Feel authentic".
- Artist programme ("Create with AVRANA"): "Your art. Worn by others." / "Made by you. Worn by others." Artists apply;
  AVRANA handles production, fulfilment and customer service; selected collaborators earn from eligible sales under
  arrangements agreed before launch (programme in its early stages).
- Store promises shown on the site: easy returns extended to 30 days; free gift packaging over $100; one-year
  warranty, no questions asked; customer support Mon–Fri 10am–9pm.
